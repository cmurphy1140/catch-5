# What the market leader gets wrong, September 14, 2026

Connor found *Pitch – Expert AI* by NeuralPlay on the App Store: 4.7 stars, 6,800 ratings, the
established app in this category. A dated snapshot of what its reviews say, kept because it changed
how we rank our own work.

## The finding

Its most-cited complaint, sustained across at least four years of reviews, is that the computer
plays badly — and specifically that **your partner** plays badly.

| Review | Stars |
|---|---|
| "Good game but ai and the counting isn't always accurate" — *"Sometimes the ai plays my partners cards like he's never played pitch before."* | 4 |
| "I WANT A NEW PARTNER !" — *"I could like this game, but the AI is off."* | 3 |
| "Great game but AI is Uber dumb" | 4 |

The developer's replies say "We are working on AI improvements" in 2022, in 2023, and again on
September 5, 2026. Four years of the same sentence.

## Why it matters here

Connor named bad computer decisions as this game's biggest gap versus his real table, independently
and before seeing any of this. The loudest complaint in the category is the same one. That is where
a small app can beat a large one, because it is the hard part rather than the wide part.

It also validates **partner instructions** (spec E.6). A review titled "I WANT A NEW PARTNER!" is a
player asking for that feature in as many words.

## Not behind on features

They ship trick-by-trick review, hints, undo, statistics, a replayable hand, and real-time guidance
when your play differs from the computer's. Catch 5 already has the first four and tap-to-explain
covers the last. The gap is not the feature list.

## Worth taking

- **Claim the remaining tricks** when a hand is unbeatable, instead of playing them out.
- **Replay a hand** to practise it, using the replay log we already keep.

## Worth avoiding

- **Rule customisation as a list.** Theirs runs to roughly twenty toggles: kitty size, stick the
  dealer, off-jack, jokers, scoring trumps, last trick. A warning for Plug & Pitch, whose whole
  premise is presets: a preset is a way to play, a toggle list is homework.
- **A built-in counter that counts for you.** Ours is deliberately manual (spec E.7, R4, R20): the
  counting is the skill.
- **Promising improvements in review replies.** Four years of the same answer is worse than silence.

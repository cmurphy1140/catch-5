Catch 5 on your iPhone

Connor's card game, installed for free outside the App Store. It takes about 30 minutes, once. Do it on a FaceTime call with Connor, or use Ask for help at the bottom of the screen.

Before you start

  • Your Mac and your iPhone, both on Wi-Fi
  • The cable that charges your iPhone
  • Your iPhone passcode
  • Your Apple Account email and password

The Apple Account is used to sign Catch 5 for your phone. It doesn't have to be your main one; a spare free Apple Account works too.

On your Mac

  1. Download iloader

    Go to github.com/nab138/iloader/releases and download the Mac file ending in .dmg from the newest release. Open it and drag iloader into Applications.

    If your Mac says it can't be opened: System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway.

  2. Plug your iPhone into the Mac

    When your iPhone asks, tap Trust and enter your passcode.

  3. Open iloader and sign in

    Use your Apple Account. Capital letters matter.

  4. Install SideStore

    Pick your iPhone, then click Install SideStore (Stable). Wait until it says it's finished. SideStore is the app that keeps Catch 5 working.

On your iPhone

  1. Trust the app

    Open Settings › General › VPN & Device Management. Under Developer App, tap your Apple Account, then Trust, then Trust again.

    If you see Allow & Restart instead, tap it and enter your passcode.

  2. Turn on Developer Mode

    Open Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll to the very bottom, and switch on Developer Mode. The phone restarts. When it's back, tap Turn On and enter your passcode.

    iPhones on iOS 16 or newer need this. If it isn't there on iOS 16 or newer, stop here and call Connor. (On iOS 15 there's no Developer Mode; skip this step.)

  3. Get LocalDevVPN

    Search the App Store for LocalDevVPN and install it. It's free. Open it, tap Connect, and allow it when your iPhone asks to add a VPN.

    It only talks to your own phone. SideStore needs it switched on to install or renew apps.

  4. Open SideStore once

    Sign in with the same Apple Account. Go to My Apps and tap the 7 DAYS button next to SideStore. If it asks about a certificate, tap Yes or Refresh Now.

    Your phone may jump back to the Home Screen. That's normal; SideStore is ready again in a few seconds.

Add Catch 5

  1. Save the file Connor sends

    Connor sends you a file called CatchFive.ipa. Open it on your iPhone and choose Save to Files.

  2. Install it with SideStore

    Make sure LocalDevVPN says Connected. In SideStore, open My Apps, tap + at the top, and pick CatchFive.ipa. When it finishes, Catch 5 is on your Home Screen. Deal yourself in.

Keeping it working

Apps installed this way last 7 days, SideStore included. SideStore renews itself and Catch 5 in the background while you're on Wi-Fi and LocalDevVPN is on.

To renew by hand: open LocalDevVPN and tap Connect, then in SideStore's My Apps tap the days number next to SideStore, then the one next to Catch 5.

If SideStore itself won't open, it ran out and can't renew itself. Plug your iPhone into the Mac, open iloader, and do step 4 again.

When Connor sends a new version, add it the same way as step 10.

If something goes wrong

"No Wi-Fi or VPN connection." Check that LocalDevVPN says Connected and Wi-Fi is on. Turn off any ad-blocker apps. Tap refresh again; it sometimes takes a few tries. Then close and reopen SideStore and LocalDevVPN.

It stopped working after an iPhone update. The link between SideStore and your phone may need redoing. Plug into the Mac, open iloader, and call Connor.
